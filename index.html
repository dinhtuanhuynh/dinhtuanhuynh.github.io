Research Directions

Current themes

Three themes organize much of my current work in complex geometry and value distribution theory.

01

Complex Hyperbolicity

Kobayashi hyperbolicity · Hyperbolic hypersurfaces · Hyperbolic complements

02

Nevanlinna Theory

Entire curves · Second Main Theorems · p-adic Nevanlinna theory · Defect relations

03

Pluripotential theory & Potential theory and its applications in value distribution theory

· Positive close currents · Density currents · Potential theory
