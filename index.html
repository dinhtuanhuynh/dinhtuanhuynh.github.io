Complex Geometry & Nevanlinna Theory

Dinh Tuan Huynh

Department of Mathematics · University of Education, Hue University

My research lies in complex geometry and value distribution theory, with emphasis on Nevanlinna theory, complex hyperbolicity, Second Main Theorems, holomorphic curves, and related problems in algebraic geometry.

Hue City, Vietnam dinhtuanhuynh@hueuni.edu.vn GitHub

Research Directions

Current themes

Three themes organize much of my current work in complex geometry and value distribution.

01

Complex Hyperbolicity

Kobayashi hyperbolicity · Hyperbolic hypersurfaces · Hyperbolic complements · Deformation methods

02

Nevanlinna Theory

Second Main Theorems · Hypersurfaces · Truncation · Subgeneral position · Defect relations

03

Holomorphic Curves & Complex Geometry

Entire curves · Jet differentials · Ahlfors currents · Projective and almost-complex geometry
