About

Mathematics at the interface of geometry and value distribution

My research concerns holomorphic curves, value distribution theory, and complex hyperbolicity. I am especially interested in sharp and effective forms of Second Main Theorems, geometric applications of Nevanlinna theory, and hyperbolicity problems in projective and almost-complex settings.

Research

Current themes

Nevanlinna theory

Second Main Theorems for hypersurfaces, truncation problems, subgeneral position, and quantitative value distribution.

Complex hyperbolicity

Kobayashi hyperbolicity, hyperbolic hypersurfaces and complements, deformation methods, and explicit low-degree constructions.

Holomorphic curves

Entire curves in projective and arithmetic-geometric settings, with connections to Grassmannians, Shimura varieties, and almost-complex geometry.
